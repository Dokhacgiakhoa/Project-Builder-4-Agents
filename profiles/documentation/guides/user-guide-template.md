## 🚀 2. QUICK START IN 3 STEPS

### Step 1: Sign Up & Log In
1. Go to the address: **`https://[app.domain.com]`**.
2. Click **"Create a new account"** (or choose **"Log in with Google / GitHub"**).
3. Verify your email (if required) and set a secure password of at least 8 characters.

### Step 2: Set Up Your Profile (Workspace Setup)
1. Fill in your display name and organization.
2. Choose your main usage role: *Manager*, *Engineer*, or *Viewer*.
3. Choose the display style you like (Light / Dark / Minimal).

### Step 3: Create Your First Task
1. On the main navigation bar, click **"+ New Project"**.
2. Enter the basic information: project name, the goal to reach, and the deadline.
3. Click **"Confirm & Launch"**.

## 🧭 3. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES

### Feature 1: The Overview Dashboard
* **Meaning**: Where all real-time statistics about work progress, system status and important alerts are shown.
* **How to use it**:
  - View the weekly completion progress chart.
  - Filter the data by status: *Running*, *Completed*, or *Needs review*.

### Feature 2: Interact with the Smart Assistant (AI Assistant)
* **Meaning**: Helps you answer questions, analyze data and suggest solutions automatically.
* **How to use it**:
  1. Open the **"AI Assistant Chat"** tab.
  2. Type your request into the chat box (for example: *"Summarize this week's report of the engineering department"*).
  3. Press **Enter** or the send button to get a detailed answer within seconds.

### Feature 3: Export Reports & Share Data
* **Meaning**: Share your work results with colleagues or your manager with just 1 link.
* **How to use it**:
  - Click the **"Share"** button at the top right of the screen.
  - Choose the access permission: *View only* or *Allowed to edit*.
  - Click the **"Copy link"** button to send it to the people concerned.
